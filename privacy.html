Privacy Policy

Ghost Escape · Callisto Solutions · Last updated: 25 September 2026

Ghost Escape is a single-player endless chase game for Android. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with information on your device.

Who we are

Ghost Escape is published by Callisto Solutions. Contact: rahmanik1228@gmail.com

What the app stores

Ghost Escape keeps a few settings on your device only, using the phone’s local storage:

  • Your best score
  • How many diamonds you have collected
  • Whether sound is muted

This information never leaves your device. We do not have a server that receives it. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

What we do not collect

  • No account, email, or sign-in
  • No ads, ad IDs, or tracking SDKs
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or location
  • No contacts, photos, microphone, or camera
  • No sale or sharing of personal data
  • No in-app purchases or payment data

Internet permission

The Android package may list an internet permission because of the app framework. Ghost Escape does not send your score, diamonds, or any personal information to us or to third parties.

Children

Ghost Escape is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
