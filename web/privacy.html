Ghost Escape Privacy

Version 5.0.1 · 9 November 2026

Progress, flashlight finishes, records and settings are stored locally on your device. This version includes no accounts, analytics, advertising, purchases or online leaderboard. No gameplay information is transmitted to a server.

Audio uses your device output; optional vibration uses its vibrator. Clearing data or uninstalling removes progress. Android cloud backup and app-data transfer are excluded.

The app owner should provide their support contact alongside this policy when publishing it.
